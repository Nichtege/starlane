# Format of the weekly plan

- Heading: `Week <ISO week>` with the dates from Monday to Sunday.
- At most 3 items per day, each with an estimated duration (`~45 min`).
- Tasks with the nearest deadline come first. Always recompute the days left from today's date.
- Keep one buffer day per week free.
- At the end: `Open questions` (what you are missing) and `Not done last week` (from the week note).
