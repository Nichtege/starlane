# Rules for Claude and other agents in this vault

## What this vault is

A small, fictional demo vault that ships with Agentic OS. Its owner is a made-up freelance illustrator who is building
two raised vegetable beds, learning Spanish and drawing a zine called "Small Greens". None of it is about a real person.
The vault is set in week 2026-W41 (Monday 5 to Sunday 11 October 2026); read dates relative to that week.

## Folder map

| Folder | Holds |
|---|---|
| `index.md` | the entry point; links every project, area and the current week |
| `AI/brain-map.md` | the second entry point: every important note with its size |
| `Projects/` | one note per project (`status`, `deadline`), plus `raised-beds-schedule.md` with tasks per week |
| `Areas/` | ongoing areas without an end date: `garden.md`, `spanish.md` |
| `Journal/` | one note per ISO week, e.g. `2026-W41.md` |
| `Resources/` | knowledge notes; `Resources/Templates/` holds the week template |
| `Inbox/` | unsorted notes; `Inbox/README.md` explains the inbox |
| `Ideas/` | `README.md` with a `## Quick` list of ideas |
| `AI/` | notes written by agents and sessions; `AI/Digest/` is generated by `aos digest` |

## How to find things

1. Start at `index.md`, then `AI/brain-map.md`. Every note is at most two links from one of them (the two-hop rule).
2. Follow the `[[wikilinks]]` from there; search with Grep only when the links do not lead to the answer.
3. Read notes section by section if one is ever large; the brain map lists the sizes.

## Frontmatter

- `type`: `project`, `plan`, `area`, `week`, `knowledge`, `session`, `overview`, `note` or `index`.
- `created`: the day the note was made (`YYYY-MM-DD`).
- `status` (projects): `idea`, `new`, `building`, `active`, `paused` or `done`. `deadline`: `YYYY-MM-DD`.
- `project`, `projects` or `area`: what a note belongs to, as a link such as `"[[Projects/raised-beds]]"`.
- `tags`: a short list.

Notes under `AI/` were written by agents or summarise a session. Each says what it belongs to, in the frontmatter and in
a `Part of [[...]]` line below the heading. Treat them as hints, not as the owner's own words.

## Dates and the weekly goal

- Dates in open list items and headings are appointments: `2026-11-28`, `28.11.2026` or a weekday with day.month
  such as `Sat 17.10.` Dates under headings like `## Log`, `## Done` or `## Status` are history.
- The week note `Journal/<year>-W<week>.md` holds `## Weekly goal` (checkboxes) and `## Weekly review` (a short honest
  look back at the end of the week).

## Rules for agents

- Read only. Never create, edit, move or delete a note unless the owner explicitly asks for that change.
- Cite the note path (for example `Projects/raised-beds.md`) for every fact you take from the vault.
- If the notes do not answer a question, say so instead of guessing.
- Never write passwords, keys or other credentials into a note.

## Good first questions to ask this vault

1. What are my active projects and when are they due?
2. What is my weekly goal for 2026-W41, and did I reach it?
3. What do I have to do in the garden between 12 and 18 October?
4. How deep and how far apart should I plant garlic, and what should not grow next to it?
5. When do I use "ser" and when "estar"?
