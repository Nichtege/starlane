# Spanish: ser or estar

Both mean "to be". The question is whether it describes what something *is* or how and where it *is right now*.

- **ser**: identity, origin, material, time. *El huerto es pequeño.* (The patch is small.) *Son las tres.*
- **estar**: location, state, result of a change. *Las semillas están en la lata.* *La tierra está mojada.*
- Trap: *ser listo* is to be clever, *estar listo* is to be ready.
