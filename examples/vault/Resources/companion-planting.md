# Companion planting

Plants that help each other, and pairs to keep apart. Rules of thumb, not laws.

| Plant | Good next to | Keep away from |
|---|---|---|
| Garlic | carrots, lettuce, strawberries | beans, peas |
| Broad beans | lettuce, spinach, potatoes | garlic, onions |
| Marigolds | tomatoes, beans | (none known) |

For the raised beds: garlic and broad beans each get their own bed. Planting details: [[Resources/garlic-planting]].
