# Planting garlic

- When: October to November, before the ground freezes. Garlic needs a cold spell to form bulbs.
- How: break the bulb into cloves, plant pointy end up, 5 cm deep, 15 cm apart, rows 25 cm apart.
- Soil: loose and well drained; raised beds are ideal. No fresh manure.
- Care: keep weeds down, water only in a dry spring, remove flower stalks in early summer.
- Harvest: June to July, when the lower third of the leaves has turned yellow. Dry for two weeks.
