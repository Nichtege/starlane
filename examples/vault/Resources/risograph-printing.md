# Risograph printing

- Every colour is its own layer and its own master: draw each colour as a separate black-and-white file.
- Expect 1 to 2 mm of misregistration between layers; avoid hairline fits, overlap shapes a little instead.
- Large solid areas smudge and cost ink. Use textures or 80 % tints.
- Uncoated paper of 100 to 120 g/m² takes the ink well; let the sheets rest before folding.
- Two colours (green and black) keep the price low for a run of 100.
