# Frost fleece: which weight?

Heard at the garden centre: light fleece (about 17 g/m²) gives roughly 2 °C of protection and lets most light
through; heavy fleece (30 g/m²) protects more but darkens the bed.

For garlic, light fleece is probably enough. Decide before the first frost and add it to [[Projects/raised-beds]].
